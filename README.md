Blackjack
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A terminal-based Blackjack game written in Python. Play against the dealer with a standard 52-card deck, make smart decisions on when to hit or stand, and try to beat the house without going bust.
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Features
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Full 52-card deck (4 suits × 13 cards), shuffled each game
Accurate Ace handling — counts as 11, drops to 1 to avoid a bust
Dealer AI — automatically hits until reaching a score of 17 or above
Live score display after every action
Input validation — handles unexpected user input gracefully
Clear win/loss/tie outcome at the end of each game
---------------------------------------------------------------------
How to Play
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The deal — you and the dealer are each dealt two cards from a shuffled deck.
Your turn — on each turn you'll see your hand and score, then choose:
hit — draw another card from the deck.
stand — end your turn and let the dealer play.
Bust — if your score exceeds 21 at any point, you lose immediately.
Dealer's turn — the dealer reveals their hand and keeps drawing until they reach a score of 17 or higher.
Outcome — scores are compared and the winner is announced.
